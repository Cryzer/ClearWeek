# ClearWeek

**Your weekly quests in World of Warcraft: Midnight, as one short list ordered by where you are.**

ClearWeek answers one question: *what is left this week, and where do I go next?* It shows only what your character can still do, puts the place you are standing in at the top, and takes you to the next step with a single click.

## What it does

- **One list, grouped by place.** Silvermoon City, Silvermoon Court, Amani Tribe, Hara'ti, The Singularity and the rest. The group for your current zone moves to the top.
- **Click to go.** Click a row and the game sets a waypoint on the quest giver, the quest objective or the turn-in.
- **Reads the game, saves no progress.** Quest state comes straight from the game on every refresh, so there is nothing to set up and nothing to tick off by hand.
- **Stays out of the way.** At rest it is a few lines next to the quest tracker: where you are, the next step and the time to the weekly reset. Hover to see the whole week.
- **Disappears when you are busy.** Hidden in combat, in dungeons, raids, delves and PvP, and back afterwards.
- **Done means gone.** Finished quests leave the list. When nothing is left you get "Week clear" and the list goes away until the reset.

## What it tracks

Weekly content only — no dailies, no world quest spam.

- **Silvermoon City:** Unity Against the Void, the weekly dungeon quest, the weekly bonus event, Trailing Xal'atath, Vaeli's weekly, Void Assaults and the Nebulous Voidcore bonus roll.
- **Prey:** A Nightmarish Task.
- **Silvermoon Court:** Favor of the Court and Fortify the Runestones.
- **Amani Tribe:** Abundant Offerings, with the current Abundance event.
- **Hara'ti:** Lost Legends and the weekly relic.
- **The Singularity:** Research Console: Exploring the Void.
- **Slayer's Duellum:** Preparing for Battle.
- **Zul'jarra's Forces:** Turn Back the Surge, with a countdown to the next Curse Surge, and Purging the Vaults.
- **World boss** of the week, listed under its zone.
- **Delves:** Trovehunter's Bounty.

A quest is listed only when your character meets its requirements (level, renown, earlier quests), so a fresh alt sees a shorter list.

## Reading the list

- **Gold** — the place you are in.
- **White** — a quest to pick up.
- **Blue-grey** — a quest in progress.
- **Green** — ready to turn in.

Other places use dimmer shades of the same colours.

## Controls

- **Click a row** — waypoint to that quest.
- **Right-click a row** — hide it for this week, or always.
- **Click the icon**, or type `/clearweek` — show or hide the list.
- **Right-click the icon** — restore hidden rows, unlock the position to drag the list, change the scale, or reset the position.

By default the list sits beside the quest tracker and follows it when you move the tracker in Edit Mode. On the left half of the screen the layout mirrors itself.

## Good to know

- **English only.** Interface text, NPC names and place names are in English on every client. Quest titles from your quest log use your game language.
- **Light on the UI.** ClearWeek does not modify the quest tracker, the world map or any Blizzard frame, and changes no game settings.
- **WaypointUI** is supported but not required: with it installed, map pins show the quest name.

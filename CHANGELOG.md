# Changelog

## 0.14.1

First public version.

- Lists this week's quests grouped by place; the group for your current zone moves to the top.
- Click a row to set a waypoint on the quest giver, the objective or the turn-in.
- Shows the weekly world boss, Abundance, the bonus roll and the next Curse Surge.
- Right-click a row to hide it for the week or for good; right-click the icon to restore rows, move or scale the list.
- Sits next to the quest tracker by default and mirrors itself on the left half of the screen.
- Hides in combat, in instances and while addons are restricted.
- `/cw` or the icon shows or hides the list.
- English only.
